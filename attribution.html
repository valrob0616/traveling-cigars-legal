Content Attribution & Takedowns

Effective October 9, 2026 · Traveling Cigars

Who owns the content

News articles, press releases, photographs, and videos belong to their publishers and creators. Traveling Cigars does not claim ownership of them.

What the App shows

  • News: headlines, source names, dates, and short index text from publishers' public RSS feeds, with a link to read the full story on the publisher's own site. We do not republish full articles.
  • Videos: public YouTube channels, including CigarTV, played through the standard YouTube embedded player. Views and playback stay on YouTube's player.
  • Places: a directory of publicly known lounges and shops with names, addresses, short notes, and map directions. No links to shop websites.

Listing, linking, or embedding is not an endorsement, and does not imply any affiliation, sponsorship, or approval by the owner.

Takedowns and corrections

If you own content shown in the App and want it removed or corrected, or if a headline or listing is wrong, email info@travelingcigars.com with the item, the requested change, and your contact details. We review requests promptly and remove or correct items where appropriate.
