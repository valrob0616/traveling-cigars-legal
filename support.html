Getting in

Traveling Cigars is for adults 21 and older. On first launch, enter your date of birth and accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. No account is needed to read News, Videos, Learn, or Places.

Accounts and deleting your account

An account is optional and only stores a name, an email address, and your favorites. To delete it, open the Account tab, tap Delete account, and confirm. This deletes your account and favorites from your device and from our server. You can also email us to request deletion.

Report a listing correction

If a lounge or shop listing is wrong, moved, or closed, email info@travelingcigars.com with the listing name, city, and the correction. You can also use Report a listing correction in the App's Legal section.

Content takedowns

Publishers and creators can request removal or correction of a headline, link, or video by emailing info@travelingcigars.com.
