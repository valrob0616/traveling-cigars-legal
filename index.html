Traveling Cigars

Cigar news, culture, education, and a U.S. lounge directory, for adults 21 and older.

Traveling Cigars is a journalistic iPhone app. It indexes headlines from public cigar-industry news feeds and links to the publishers, plays videos from public YouTube channels through the standard YouTube player, explains vocabulary, history, and geography in a Beginner's Index, and lists publicly known cigar lounges and tobacconist shops with map directions.

The app does not sell tobacco, shows no prices, coupons, or shop links, and does not encourage tobacco use. Tobacco use carries serious health risks.
